1 · SHENZHEN · SUMMIT

TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN MEET IN SHENZHEN

Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen on Thursday for the first three-way summit of the sitting leaders of the US, China and Russia, on the sidelines of APEC. Trump meets Xi first at 12:45 WITA; the three-way talks follow at 14:30 WITA at Kylin Villa, with one hour allocated. Nuclear arms top the agenda after New START expired in February 2026, alongside Ukraine, the war with Iran, trade and AI, but no agreement is expected. [NYT]

2 · MIDDLE EAST · GAZA

ISRAELI TANKS PUSH ON IN GAZA

The operation began Wednesday afternoon; Thursday’s news is the continuing push onto Salah al-Din road east of Nuseirat and US CENTCOM chief Admiral Brad Cooper’s visit to Israel. The IDF says it is dismantling a tunnel west of the Yellow Line. Three Palestinians were killed in separate strikes on Wednesday; Hamas calls the incursion a “flagrant” violation. [BBC]

3 · BUSINESS · TECH

ANTHROPIC DEBUTS ON NASDAQ

Shares begin trading Thursday as ANT after pricing at $83 on Wednesday. The $81.3 billion raised across 980 million shares makes it the largest IPO on record, ahead of SpaceX’s $75 billion debut. Valued at roughly $1.5 trillion fully diluted, Anthropic is the first frontier AI lab to list. [Reuters]

4 · EUROPE · UKRAINE WAR

CRIMEAN BRIDGE ATTACKED AGAIN

The head of Crimea says traffic was suspended after another attack on the link between Russia and occupied Crimea. It follows Tuesday’s strikes that dropped the 227-metre central road span into the sea. [BBC]

5 · ASIA · LAW

WORLD COURT RULES ON ROHINGYA TODAY

The ICJ delivers its judgment in The Gambia v Myanmar on Thursday. No state has ever been found guilty of genocide by an international court. The 2017 campaign killed thousands, razed nearly 400 villages and drove more than 700,000 Rohingya to Bangladesh. [BBC]

6 · CULTURE

VAN GOGH SELLS FOR RECORD $190.4M

“Châtaigniers en fleurs” sells at auction for a record $190.4 million after a row over its Nazi history. Van Gogh painted it in 1890, in the final two months of his life. [BBC]

7 · WORLD · DEMOGRAPHICS

ISLAM TO BE LARGEST FAITH BY 2035

Pew Research projects Muslims will outnumber Christians worldwide by 2035, making Islam the largest faith. Hindus will rise from 1.2 billion in 2020 to 1.4 billion by 2070. [Pew]

8 · EUROPE · EU

DEUTSCHE BANK FACES PRIVACY PROBE

Hesse’s data protection commissioner opens a probe into the leak of more than 15 million internal files, published 17 Nov. Reuters reports on the media evaluation Thursday; the bank says account numbers, balances and login credentials were unaffected, and shares fell 5.6%. [Reuters]

9 · MIDDLE EAST

Canada announces sanctions targeting Israeli settlers and organisations over West Bank expansion.

10 · ASIA-PACIFIC

INDIA AND JAPAN HOLD 2+2 TALKS

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh and External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar meet their counterparts in Tokyo, with defence and security ties at the centre.

11 · AFRICA

ETHIOPIA AND ERITREA MASS FORCES AT BORDER

ACLED and the International Crisis Group say both armies are within striking distance, fuelling fears of outright war. ReliefWeb’s Atrocity Alert No. 503, updated 19 Nov, warns of atrocity risk as clashes near disputed Badme continue. [Reuters]

12 · AMERICAS

The United States and Venezuela agree to restore military cooperation, including training and intelligence sharing, US Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth says.
